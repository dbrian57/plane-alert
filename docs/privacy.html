Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 16, 2026

plane-alert ("the application") is a personal-use notification tool built and operated by a single individual (the "account holder"). It sends the account holder an SMS text message when an aircraft is detected near a location the account holder has configured. This application has exactly one recipient: the account holder. It is not a public service and does not collect information from, or send messages to, any other person.

Information We Collect

The only phone number used by this application is the account holder's own number, entered directly by the account holder into the application's private configuration. No other personal information is collected from any user, and no sign-up form or public intake process exists.

How We Use Information

The account holder's phone number is used solely to deliver automated SMS alerts generated by the application when it detects aircraft near the account holder's configured coordinates.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share the account holder's mobile phone number, or any other personal information, with any third party. The phone number is not shared with affiliates, marketing partners, data brokers, or any other organization. It is used exclusively to deliver the SMS notifications described above.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies with aircraft activity near the configured location and is not fixed. On a typical day this may range from zero messages to several messages.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply.

Opt-Out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving further notifications. Reply HELP for assistance.

Data Retention

The application stores minimal state (a per-aircraft timestamp used to avoid duplicate alerts) locally, for its own operation. This data is not sold, shared, or used for any purpose other than operating the notification cooldown described above.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
